## 9. TREND: PATTERN CHANGE (2024 vs OLDER PAPERS)

| Feature | 2019–2023 papers | 2024 papers |
|---------|-----------------|-------------|
| Total marks | 60 | 100 |
| Duration | 2.5 hours | 3 hours |
| Long essay | 12 marks (3 of 4) | 10 marks (2 of 3) |
| Short notes | 10 marks (5 of 6) | 12 marks (3 of 4) — 4m each |
| Reasoning/brief | 8 marks (one-two lines) | 18 marks (2m each) — 9 of 10 |
| Sections | 2 sections | 2 sections with subsections |
| Psychiatry/Derm/TB | Separate section II (Paper-II) | Integrated in Paper-II Q4/Q5/Q6 |
| Objective (1m) | Not present | Present (Paper-II Q4C, Q5C, Q6C) |
| Clinical vignette | Not standard | Present in 2024 Paper-I Q1.1 and Paper-II Q1.1 |
